# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What that means here

**Do:**

- Ask questions. Nobody was born knowing how Telegram's rate limits work.
- Say when something is unclear — that is a documentation bug, and reporting it
  helps the next person.
- Disagree about technical decisions. That is how things improve.
- Give people the benefit of the doubt when a message reads badly. Most people
  here are writing in a second language, in a hurry, on a phone.

**Don't:**

- Insult people, or their code, or their level of experience.
- Make it personal when the argument is technical.
- Harass anyone, publicly or privately.
- Post anyone's personal information.
- Keep pushing a point after a decision has been made and explained.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through a GitHub security advisory on this repository, or by
opening an issue if it is not sensitive. Reports are read by the maintainer and
nobody else.

Consequences depend on what happened: usually a request to stop, sometimes
removing a comment, and for anything serious or repeated, a block.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else connected
to this project.
